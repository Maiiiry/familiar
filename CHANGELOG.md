# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-17

The first release of Familiar: a reading tracker with a pixel-art familiar that feeds on stories.

- **Eight familiars** in rich pixel art painted on the phone: the mimic, kitsune and slime (the three you can hatch first), and the dragon, phoenix, griffin, owlbear and moon moth (found by reading). Five stages from egg to ancient, each familiar with its own favourite kind of reading.
- **They show what you read:** the kitsune grows a tail for every genre, the dragon sits on a hoard of finished books, the moth's wings fill with a star for every night, the slime keeps what you named inside.
- **The spell of forgetting:** two quiet days and the familiar starts turning to stone from the paws up; the phoenix burns to ash instead. A few pages break the spell.
- **The ritual:** a candle that burns while you read, a page counter with a rustle, a synthesised fireplace or rain, and a stillness bonus while the phone lies face down. Or log pages read elsewhere.
- **The feast** after each sitting and moods after each genre.
- **Find a book** in Open Library (Google Books as a fallback): title, author, pages and the real cover, kept on the phone. Or a cover drawn in the genre's colour.
- **The tree of stories**, three monthly guild errands, the saga of your year, an evening reminder told by the familiar, a home-screen widget, JSON backup, English and Russian, parchment and candlelight themes.
